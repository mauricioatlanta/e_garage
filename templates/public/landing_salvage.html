The story behind the software

"After years working in a real salvage yard in Atlanta, GA, we built the system we needed and couldn't find anywhere."

We searched for software to manage parts inventory — and found nothing that understood how a salvage yard actually works.

Generic systems treat each part as a standalone item. But a 2015 Camry coming in is 200 parts with different origins, conditions, and prices. Nobody modeled it that way. So we built it ourselves.

eGarage Salvage was born in a real salvage yard in the USA. Now we open it to all of Latin America and beyond.
